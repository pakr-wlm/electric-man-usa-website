Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work on homes and businesses?

Both. We do residential and commercial work, from remodels and panel changes at home to tenant build outs and meter pack replacements for businesses.

Do you do landscape lighting?

Yes. We design indoor and outdoor landscape lighting for local designers in Atlanta and the surrounding areas.

Are your technicians licensed?

Yes. Every technician is vetted and fully licensed.

What forms of payment do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard and American Express.

Can you fix problems flagged in a home inspection?

Yes. Real estate electrical inspection corrections and code upgrades are both on our service list.

Is it safe to keep using a breaker that keeps tripping?

Not for long. A breaker trips to protect the wiring from overload or a fault. If it keeps happening, turn off what is on that circuit and have it checked.
