# Electric Man USA: homepage

Free homepage rebuild for Electric Man USA (Atlanta, GA), from electricmanusa.com. Ship `index.html`: one self-contained file (CSS and images inlined). Edit `index.src.html` and `styles.css`, then run `python build.py`.

## Real data
- **Google rating:** 5.0 across 15 reviews, read from their Google Maps profile (Sept 2026). Also in the JSON-LD.
- **Reviews:** 6 verbatim Google reviews (zach cohen, Kevin Patterson, Danielle Reid, marsi blecher, S W, Katie Ferguson). Ten of the 15 were readable; the other reviews are not used. Most are 5 years old; the newest is 8 months old.
- **From their own site:** phone (770) 986-9501, email electric.maninc@yahoo.com (used by the quote form), hours Mon-Fri 8am-5pm, "over 20 years in business", "vetted and fully licensed" technicians, the residential and commercial service lists, landscape lighting design for local designers, Visa/Mastercard/Amex.
- **Photos:** all real, from their own website. Hero (ring chandelier), About (crew and vans), fleet strip, and the 8 Recent Work tiles are from their /gallery page.
- **Logo and colors:** logo from their site, cropped to a circle. Black and yellow sampled from its pixels.
